# Capabilities: gold hardware art direction

Created October 15, 2026 with the built-in ImageGen tool. No external provider imagery, models, videos, or runtime dependencies were added. The artwork is conceptual, not a product screenshot, live telemetry, or an integrated provider's hardware.

## Direction selection

Seven generations explored three directions: cinematic orbital infrastructure, an exploded precision instrument, and a sculptural orbital instrument. `cinematic-layout-reference.png` is the generated layout reference. The final hero follows its large left-aligned ivory grotesk type, open dark left field, realistic spacecraft at right, blue Earth limb, and tactile gold action. The exploded study explains the abstraction below the request. The sculpture closes the narrative. Independent transparent parts allow layer selection and subtle physical motion without covering their detail with UI boxes.

## Final prompt set

Shared direction: photorealistic product rendering, gold/brass with sapphire solar cells and midnight/Klein-blue background, technically plausible conceptual space hardware, controlled studio reflections, no provider trademarks or invented operational values.

1. **Layout reference.** Single large high-fidelity desktop section for Nomos Orbital space infrastructure software, 1536×1024. White slim header, left-third ivory grotesk headline “One request. The whole space stack.”, copy “Intelligence across orbital, ground, and compute infrastructure.”, tactile polished gold “Run a request” button. Right two-thirds realistic gold-foil spacecraft above curved blue Earth, ground antenna and compute infrastructure as physical miniatures. Restrained optical connection paths, cinematic rim lighting, real material imperfections. No UI cards, dashboards, wireframe circles, cartoon illustration, or fake metrics. Caption: conceptual infrastructure illustration.
2. **Exploded instrument.** Photorealistic 1536×1024 product artwork, no text or UI. Dark cobalt studio. Four physical parts along a diagonal: brushed gold circular base and connector pins, sapphire processor wafer with gold traces, detailed insulated spacecraft bus, parabolic gold antenna. Black anodized structure, foil folds, machined brass rings and studio glints. Middle/right subject, left negative space. Credible engineering, not science-fiction toys.
3. **Orbital sculpture.** Photorealistic cinematic 1536×1024 artwork, no text or logos. Gold armillary instrument around deep cobalt Earth sphere, thick machined gimbal arcs, small spacecraft on an arm, miniature ground dish and compute core at base. Right 65% object, left dark negative space. Gold reflections, engraved microtexture without letters, quiet volumetric blue light. Editorial metaphor, not telemetry.
4. **Hero background.** Photorealistic 1536×1024, no text or UI. Right-half detailed gold-foil Earth-observation satellite and diagonal sapphire arrays; lower third blue Earth limb, clouds and city lights. Subtle antenna/infrastructure at lower right. Left 45% dark sky for type. Natural thermal-foil folds, screws, mesh antenna and precise solar cells. Controlled glints, no excessive flare or stars.
5. **Satellite cutout.** Transparent isolated complete satellite, square composition with padding, three-quarter front view. Gold multilayer insulation, black aerospace frame, blue solar arrays with gold traces, dish and optical aperture. Warm rim/cool fill, physically based studio product rendering. No text, logo, floor, diagram or background. Conceptual hardware, not a named real model.
6. **Ground cutout.** Transparent isolated complete parabolic ground antenna. Three-quarter view, gold mesh dish, gold feed horn, navy anodized support, machined gold mount and compact circular foot. Detailed engineering, brushed metal and glossy golden edge highlights. Warm rim/cool fill. No text, logo, floor or background.
7. **Compute cutout.** Transparent isolated aerospace edge-computing module. Sapphire circular silicon wafer and gold microchip, gold circuit traces through layered dark-blue ceramic PCB, pin connectors and sculpted radial gold heat sink. Three-quarter isometric perspective, brushed brass and mirror-gold highlights, warm rim/cool fill. No text, labels, diagram, floor or background.

## Motion and proof

Layer controls select the relevant object and explanation. Bounded fine-pointer tilt and slow suspension convey physical depth. A native pause control stops motion; offscreen/background suspension and reduced-motion fallbacks conserve work. No autoplay scene changes. Evidence and unavailable-provider disclosures remain real text and unchanged in meaning.
